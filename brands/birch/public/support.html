Common questions

My Birch order arrived damaged. Where can I ask for a refund?

Use Contact Birch support above. Explain what arrived damaged and ask the team to review your refund request. A request is not confirmation of a refund.

Where can I get help with a Birch delivery?

Contact the same support team with your delivery question and order reference.

How can I contact Birch about my account?

Use our help centre or contact support. Never share your password.
